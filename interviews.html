User Interviews

👤 User #1: Casual Peloton Rider

Experience Level: Uses the Peloton twice a week for the past three months.

🚴 Typical Routine

  • Logs in and selects "Just Ride"
  • Rides for 10 miles without taking structured classes

⚠️ Challenges

  • Never connected headphones before.
  • Didn't realize Peloton had structured classes.

💡 Suggested Improvements

  • Feature Discovery: Add a tutorial pop-up for first-time users.
  • Easier Navigation: One-click Bluetooth setup for headphones.
  • Saved Preferences: Auto-save seat height and resistance settings.

👤 User #2: Experienced but Infrequent User

Experience Level: Used to ride every day, now uses it occasionally.

🎯 Expectations Before First Ride

Thought it was just a stationary bike, surprised by music-matched instructor workouts.

🚴 Routine

  • Logs in, searches by playlist or instructor.
  • Connects headphones, starts the ride.

⚠️ Challenges

  • Bluetooth connection issues.
  • Seat adjustment problems.

⭐ Favorite Features

  • Leaderboards, metrics, and gamified progress.

💡 Suggestions

  • More intuitive bike customization.
  • One-click Bluetooth pairing.

👤 User #3: First-Time Peloton User

Experience Level: New to Peloton, used basic stationary bikes before.

🎯 Expectations

Expected a simple bike, impressed by the digital experience.

🚴 Routine

  • Logs in, filters classes by duration and music.
  • Chooses 20–30 min beginner rides.

⚠️ Challenges

  • Adjusting seat height.
  • Understanding performance metrics.
  • Overwhelmed by class choices.

⭐ Favorites

  • Instructor music, leaderboards, post-ride stats.

💡 Improvements

  • Pre-ride tutorial for new users.
  • Quick-start ride button.
  • More intuitive seat adjustment.

🚀 Final Thoughts

Would recommend Peloton to new users.
